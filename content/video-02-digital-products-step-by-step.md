Koi audience nahi chahiye. Koi paisa nahi chahiye. Sirf agle 8 minute aur ek weekend.

### [0:15–0:45] SETUP & PROBLEM
*[VISUAL: confused banda 20 browser tabs ke saath, phir ek clean single roadmap]*
*[VO: warm, slower — relatable]*

Dekho, problem ye nahi hai ki log mehnat nahi karte. Problem ye hai ki shuruaat kahan se karein samajh nahi aata. "Kya bechun? Kahan bechun? Log kharidenge bhi?"

Yahi confusion mein 90% log ruk jaate hain. Isi liye aaj koi theory nahi — sirf ek seedha 5-step roadmap. Step by step. End tak aapke paas ek real, bikne-layak product hoga.

Chaliye step one se shuru karte hain — sabse important step.

---

### [0:45–2:15] STEP 1 — IDEA: SOLVE EK CHHOTI PROBLEM
*[VISUAL: lightbulb, phir 3 example product covers — "Resume Template", "Excel Budget Tracker", "Instagram Reels Presets"]*
*[VO: energetic — "ye aap aaj soch sakte ho"]*

Step one: idea. Aur yahin most log galti karte hain — wo "bada" idea dhoondhte hain. Galat. Sabse zyada bikne wala digital product wo hota hai jo *ek chhoti, irritating problem* solve kare.

Formula simple hai: **aapko kya aata hai + logon ko kis cheez mein dikkat hoti hai.**

Examples? Agar Excel aata hai — ek budget ya expense tracker banao. Agar acche resume bana lete ho — ek resume template. Agar editing aati hai — Instagram Reels ke presets ya thumbnail pack. College notes, wedding planner checklist, diet chart, freelancer ke liye invoice template — sab digital products hain.

*[VISUAL: notebook pe likha "Skill → Problem → Product"]*

Yahin pe abhi pause karo aur 5 ideas likho. Sabse simple wala chuno — kyunki simple banane mein aasaan, bechne mein aasaan. Ye aapka **digital product** hai.

*[transition]* Idea mil gaya? Ruko — bechne se pehle ek cheez confirm karni hai.

---

### [2:15–3:45] STEP 2 — VALIDATE: pehle confirm, phir banao
*[VISUAL: YouTube search bar, Etsy/Gumroad search results, comments section scroll]*
*[VO: steady — "smart log pehle check karte hain"]*

Step two: validation. Most log seedha banana shuru kar dete hain aur 2 hafte baad pata chalta hai kisi ko chahiye hi nahi tha. Smart move: *pehle demand check karo, phir banao.*

Ye 10 minute ka kaam hai. Teen jagah dekho.

*[VISUAL: search screens]*

Ek — YouTube pe apne idea se related search karo. Agar logon ke videos aur sawaal hain, matlab interest hai. Do — Gumroad ya Etsy pe same type ka product search karo. Agar pehle se bik raha hai — ye *acchi* khabar hai. Competition matlab paying customers hain. Teen — Reddit ya Instagram comments padho, jahan log apni problem ki shikayat kar rahe hain.

Most people don't realise — koi sale na hona "free" nahi hota, "no demand" hota hai. *[pause]* Validation aapko hafton ki barbaadi se bachata hai.

Demand dikh gayi? Perfect. Ab banate hain — aur ye sabse aasaan part hai.

---

### [3:45–5:15] STEP 3 — CREATE: free tools se banao
*[VISUAL: Canva, Google Sheets, Notion, Google Docs logos; screen recording of a template being built]*
*[VO: grounded, practical]*

Step three: creation. Aur ₹0 mein. Aapko coding nahi aati toh bhi chalega.

*[VISUAL: tool-by-tool]*

**Canva** (free) — templates, planners, e-books, social media kits, presets. Drag and drop, bas.
**Google Sheets ya Excel** — trackers, calculators, budgets.
**Google Docs / Notion** — guides, checklists, notes, templates. Notion templates aajkal bohot bikte hain.
E-book bana rahe ho? Docs mein likho, "Download as PDF" — ho gaya.

Ek rule: product ko *finished aur clean* dikhna chahiye. Ek acchi cover image banao Canva mein — kyunki online log cover dekh ke kharidte hain, content baad mein.

*[VISUAL: simple before/after of an ugly vs clean cover]*

Aur perfectionism mein mat phaso. Version 1 "kaafi accha" hona chahiye, perfect nahi. Customer feedback se aap usse improve karte raho — wahi product time ke saath zyada paisa kamata hai. Yahi **passive income** ka asli engine hai: ek baar banao, baar baar becho.

*[transition]* Product ready hai. Ab ise paise kamane wali cheez banate hain — store set up.

---

### [5:15–6:30] STEP 4 — SELL: store set up karo (5 minute)
*[VISUAL: Gumroad, Topmate, Instamojo dashboards; "Upload → Price → Publish" steps]*
*[VO: faster, punchy — momentum]*

Step four: bechna kahan. India ke liye teen best, sab free to start —

**Gumroad** — global, simplest. File upload karo, price set karo, link mil jaata hai. Bik gaya toh file khud chali jaati hai.
**Topmate** — India mein creators ke beech popular, UPI/Indian payments smooth.
**Instamojo** — fully Indian, UPI aur payment links ke saath.

Setup literally 5 minute: account banao, file upload, cover image lagao, price daalo, publish. Bas. Ab aapke paas ek link hai jo 24/7 bik sakta hai — aap so rahe ho tab bhi.

*[VISUAL: pricing slider]*
*[VO: slow down on numbers]*

Pricing? Pehla product zyada mehnga mat rakho. ₹149 se ₹499 ka sweet spot hai shuruaat ke liye. 100 log ₹299 mein khareeden — wo ₹29,900. *[pause]* Aur agla 100 bechne mein aapको koi naya kaam nahi karna. Yahi **make money online** ka magic hai.

---

### [6:30–7:30] STEP 5 — FIRST SALES (+ jo galtiyan sab karte hain)
*[VISUAL: Instagram Reel, YouTube short, "link in bio", first-sale notification]*
*[VO: sincere, the real-talk section]*

Step five — aur ye wo step hai jahan log haar maan lete hain. Product upload karke baith jaate hain aur sochte hain sales apne aap aayengi. *[pause]* Nahi aayengi.

Pehli sales ke liye aapko product *dikhana* padta hai. Audience nahi hai? Koi baat nahi — yahan se shuru karo:

Ek — chhoti, helpful Reels ya YouTube Shorts banao jo wahi problem solve karein jo aapka product solve karta hai. Free value do, end mein product link. Do — relevant communities mein (Reddit, Telegram, LinkedIn) genuinely madad karo, spam mat karo. Teen — ek free mini-version do, full version bechne ke liye.

Ab teen galtiyan jo *zaroor* avoid karni hain: *[VISUAL: 3 red X marks]*
Ek — perfect banane ke chakkar mein kabhi launch na karna. Do — sirf ek baar promote karke chhod dena. Teen — feedback ignore karna.

Realistic baat: pehli sale mein kuch din se kuch hafte lag sakte hain. Lekin pehli ₹100 ki sale jis din aati hai — *[pause]* tab samajh aata hai ye sach mein possible hai.
